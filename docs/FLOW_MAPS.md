# Autonomous Flow Maps & Knowledge Graphs

Flow Maps provide an interactive visual workspace to explore research papers, group findings, link notes, and generate autonomous knowledge graphs.

---

## 🤖 AI-Generated Flow Maps

ResearchLoom can autonomously construct a comprehensive knowledge graph directly from your research queries or active paper candidate pools:

1. In **Flow Maps**, click the **AI Map** button on the top canvas toolbar.
2. Enter your research domain or topic (e.g., *"Self-Supervised Vision Transformers for Histopathology"*).
3. The backend evaluates key concepts, methodologies, bottlenecks, and benchmarks using your configured LLM (Gemini, Claude, Groq, or OpenAI).
4. An interconnected graph of **Core Problems**, **Methodological Paradigms**, **Key Citations**, and **Open Research Gaps** is generated and placed onto the canvas with automatic force-directed spacing.

---

## 👆 Tablet Multi-Touch & Gesture Navigation

- **One-Finger Drag**: Select and move paper cards, folder nodes, or sticky notes across the canvas.
- **Two-Finger Pan**: Drag anywhere on the background to pan the canvas viewport smoothly.
- **Two-Finger Pinch-to-Zoom**: Fluid zoom in/out with hardware acceleration.
- **Export to Stylus Studio**: Click the arrow icon on any paper card to export its abstract or key formula directly to Stylus Studio for mathematical derivation and note-taking.
